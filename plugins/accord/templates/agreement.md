# Agreement: {task}

This agreement gives trust a shape. It records the understanding reached by the
human and agent before the work begins. It names purpose, authority, and
reserved judgment without trying to predict every implementation choice.

## What matters

Why the work is worth doing. What it must accomplish and what it must not
sacrifice.

## What good looks like

The intended outcome and the evidence that will let human and agent recognize
success.

## Where to begin

The first approach. It gives the agent a reasoned starting point without binding
the work to a plan that evidence may overturn.

## Where we meet

Review points where the human keeps judgment over what comes next. For each,
name the work or evidence that will be ready to inspect, the judgment sought,
the consequential choices kept open, and what may become harder to change
afterward.

Review points are not implementation steps. A useful one arrives late enough
for the work to be judged and early enough for judgment to matter.

## What we can spend

Time, budget, tools, access, dependencies, and other resources available to the
work.

## Risks understood

Material risks the human accepts in advance so the agent can act without asking
again when one of them becomes real.

## Room to act

The bounds within which the agent owns implementation and may adapt its
approach without seeking further direction.

## Questions kept by the human

Choices that return to the human before the agent acts, including the judgment
reserved at each agreed review point.

## Amendments

Changes accepted after the work begins, with enough context to preserve what
changed and why.
