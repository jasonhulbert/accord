# Lexicon

Every term in the metaphor that carries real weight, in plain language, traced to the narrative that defines it. The narratives define; this lexicon locates. Where this file and a narrative disagree, this file is wrong.

This list is closed the same way the event taxonomy is closed: a term absent from it is color, not canon, and adding one is a canon change — a rider to the patron, never an implementation choice. The list exists so drift and unauthorized terms are catchable, not so terms can be defined more precisely than the narratives allow. No entry may carry an operational definition.

## The roles

- **Patron** — the one who decides that something beyond the horizon is worth seeking. Names the destination and why it matters, commits provisions and trust, and answers the questions that ride home. (`code/patron.md`)
- **Frontiersman** — the one hired to find a way. Accepts responsibility for the route without pretending to know it in advance. (`code/frontiersman.md`)
- **Scout** — a rider sent ahead by the frontiersman with a bounded question. Travels on the expedition's provisions and under its commission; owes a report, not an answer. (`code/scout.md`)
- **The company** — the expedition as a whole: everyone and everything traveling under the charter. (throughout)

## The country

- **Horizon** — the destination and the reason for the journey, as named from home. Direction, not a route. (`code/patron.md`, "Choose the horizon")
- **The country** (the land, the valley) — reality as actually encountered. Indifferent to confidence, authority, and expectation; every plan must answer to it. (`code/frontiersman.md`, opening)
- **The map** — what is known so far. Always incomplete; grows truer with every honest addition. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Ink the journal")
- **Blank space** — what no one has seen yet. Marked honestly rather than filled with guesses. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Trust the scouts"; `code/scout.md`, "Return with less blank space")

## Setting out

- **Expedition** — one chartered journey toward one horizon. It ends in arrival or return. (`code/patron.md`, opening)
- **Charter** — the agreement drawn before departure: purpose, destination, first approach, provisions, dangers accepted, what counts as arrival, how far the frontiersman may act alone, and which questions must ride home. Disposable; it expires with the expedition. (`code/patron.md`, "Charter the journey")
- **Provisions** — what the expedition carries and may spend: time, money, tools, trust. (`code/patron.md`, opening)
- **Departure** — the moment the expedition begins under its charter. (`code/patron.md`, "Charter the journey")

## On the trail

- **Crossing** — an attempt at part of the route. It succeeds or fails, and a failed crossing is a fact of the country, not a sin of the traveler. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Ink the journal"; `code/patron.md`, "Judge the journey")
- **Course change** — a new route taken while the purpose holds. Proof of watching the land, not of losing the way. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Keep your eyes on the horizon")
- **Basecamp** — a place to rest, gather what was learned, and prepare for what comes next. Sturdy enough for the night, never worth defending for its own sake. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Build basecamps")
- **Settlement** — what a basecamp must not become: a camp so permanent that defending it matters more than moving forward. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Build basecamps")
- **Scout report** — the land as one rider found it, witnessed kept apart from surmised. (`code/scout.md`, "Report the land")

## Word between camp and home

- **Dispatch** — the frontiersman's account sent to the patron: miles gained, crossings lost, landmarks found, the trail ahead. Bad news gains nothing from delay. (`code/patron.md`, "Read the dispatches")
- **Rider** — a question that must go home before the company acts, carried with the frontiersman's counsel. The charter says which questions ride. (`code/patron.md`, "Answer the questions that ride home")
- **Word** — the patron's answer to a rider: press on, turn aside, or return. (`code/patron.md`, "Answer the questions that ride home")

## The record

- **Journal** — the record inked while the fire still burns: wrong turns beside right ones, questions left open. A map for those who follow, never a mandate. (`code/frontiersman.md`, "Ink the journal")
- **Account** — an honest telling of what happened, wherever it appears — in a report, a dispatch, a journal, or the log. (`code/patron.md`, "Judge the journey")

## Coming home

- **Arrival** — the expedition accomplished what the charter said would count as arrival. (`code/patron.md`, "Charter the journey")
- **Return** — the expedition ends without arrival. The road home stays open to unwelcome news; a return with an honest map is not a disgrace. (`code/patron.md`, "Answer the questions that ride home"; "Judge the journey")
